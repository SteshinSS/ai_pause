One entry per symbol, anywhere in this file; headings and other text here are only for finding your way.

- under: only in formulas under a heading with this text, for a letter that means two things (k).

- input: an input's name in table.json (pause_inputs.csv or AIFM's parameters): the popup shows its value.
- value: a value in words, when it isn't an input.
- supplement: sections of AIFM's supplementary materials that discuss it, by heading id, separated by spaces: the

# Results Verification

### Splits

<Symbol tex="C_e" unit="H100e">Exploration compute: the AI's own experiments, not the re-runs.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="C_r" unit="H100e">Reproduction compute: re-runs of the checked papers' main experiments.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="N_e" unit="FTE">Staff who keep researching, as in AIFM: those with the best research taste. In Compartmentalization nobody does: $N_e \to 0$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="N_v" unit="FTE">Staff who verify papers. In Compartmentalization the staff split their time between verifying and planning, and any time left over stays idle: $N_p + N_v \le L_H$.</Symbol>

### Research

<Symbol tex="X_e" unit="AIFM research units per year">The labs' research: AIFM's throughput of their exploration compute and of the coding labor of their researchers and AI coders. In Compartmentalization, with the AI engineer's coding labor alone, at $E_{AC}$: what they would research if every round were planned. In Workload Approval, with the approved coding labor $L_C^a$ instead.</Symbol>

### Taste

<Symbol tex="z" unit="SDs">A staff member's place among human researchers, as $Z$: their taste is $T_H(z)$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\hat z" unit="SDs">The cut: staff above it keep researching, staff below it verify. It keeps the share $N_e / L_H$ of the staff, those with the best taste.</Symbol>

### Verified Research

<Symbol tex="RE_e" unit="AIFM research units per year">The labs' research effort: their research $X_e$ weighted by their taste, $T X_e$. Per paper, $RE_i$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="RE_i" unit="AIFM research units">Research effort of paper $i$: its research $r_i$ weighted by the labs' taste and by the quality of its idea, $T v_i r_i$. A year's papers add up to the labs' research effort $RE_e$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="RE^v" unit="AIFM research units per year">Verified research effort: the research effort of the papers that were both reviewed and reproduced. In Results Verification, $\varphi_h RE_e$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\varphi_h">Share of the research effort held by the papers the verifiers check, when they check the top share $q$ of the papers they choose from: $\varphi_h(q)$. In Results Verification they choose from all of the papers, and verified research effort is $\varphi_h RE_e$. In Compartmentalization they choose from the planned papers: $\varphi_h(q / q_p)$.</Symbol>

### Papers

<Symbol tex="i">A paper, numbered from 1 to $n$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="n" unit="papers per year">Papers the labs write in a year, failed projects not counted. In Compartmentalization, the papers of every round they could run: they write the planned share $q_p$ of them.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="r_i" unit="AIFM research units">Research of paper $i$: how much research went into it, whatever its idea. It grows with the paper's compute. A year's papers add up to the labs' research $X_e$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="r_s" unit="AIFM research units">Research of a 2025 paper with the average 2025 paper's compute and labor, $\bar c_s$ and $\bar l_s$. A paper of the labs with that compute holds $l_\infty$ times more.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="l_\infty" input="l_inf">A paper's labor ceiling: with all the labor it can use, as the labs' papers have from AC on, a paper holds $l_\infty$ times the research of a 2025 paper with the same compute.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="v_i">Quality of paper $i$'s idea: what a unit of its research is worth, compared with the year's average paper. Its research effort is $RE_i = T v_i r_i$: idea quality spreads the labs' taste across their papers. Pareto, averaging 1 as in 2025, independent of the paper's compute.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="v_m">The weakest idea a paper can have, the floor of the Pareto distribution: $1 - 1/\alpha_v$, as idea quality averages 1.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\alpha_v" input="alpha">Tail index of the papers' idea quality. Near 1 a few papers hold almost all of the research effort; the larger it is, the more evenly it spreads.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="c_i" unit="H100e-years">Compute spent on paper $i$, dead ends included.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="M" input="M">Compute of all projects relative to the compute of those that became papers. Some projects fail as a whole, and the papers carry them: with its share of the failed projects, paper $i$ takes $M c_i$ of the compute. The same for the 2025 field outside the AI labs and for the labs in the pause.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\bar c" unit="H100e-years">The labs' average compute per paper, dead ends included: $C_e / (M n)$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\bar c_s" unit="H100e-years">The 2025 AI safety field's average compute per paper, dead ends included: $C_s / (M n_s)$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\zeta_p" input="zeta_p">Return of a paper's research to its compute: ten times the compute gives a paper $10^{\zeta_p}$ times more research, whatever its idea.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\sigma_c" input="sigma_c">Spread of compute across a year's papers: the standard deviation of $\ln c_i$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="l_i" unit="FTE-years">Coding labor spent on paper $i$, by people and AI coders together.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\bar l_s" unit="FTE-years">The 2025 AI safety field's average coding labor per paper: its $L_C$ over $n_s$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="c_\infty">A paper's compute ceiling: with all the compute it can use, a paper holds $c_\infty$ times the research of a 2025 paper with the same labor. It drops out once papers have all the labor they can use.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\rho_p">How a paper's compute and labor substitute for each other, like $\rho_X$ for the labs. It is below 0, so each alone raises a paper's research only up to its ceiling. It drops out once papers have all the labor they can use.</Symbol>

### Review Work

<Symbol tex="t_s" unit="FTE-years">Staff time it took the 2025 field to produce a paper, all-in: $N_s / n_s$, dead ends, failed projects and reading included.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="n_s" unit="papers per year" input="n_s">Papers the 2025 AI safety field wrote in a year: the 2025 papers of our dataset.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\kappa_h" input="kappa_h">One verifier's time to check a paper relative to the time it took the 2025 field to produce one, dead ends and failed projects included, when the labs write as many papers as the 2025 field: $\tau / t_s$ at $n = n_s$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\tau" unit="FTE-years">One verifier's time to check a paper, studying its prerequisites included. It grows with the number of papers.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="p_h" unit="FTE-years" input="p_h">Time a check gains for every tenfold more papers a year than the 2025 field wrote: the verifier's time to study the papers it builds on.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="m_h" unit="verifiers" input="m_h">Verifiers who check each paper, each on their own.</Symbol>

### Paper Count

<Symbol tex="k_X">How many times the 2025 field's research the labs do: $X_e / X_s$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="s" under="Paper Count">In $\mathbb{E}_s$: an average over the 2025 field's papers, where $\mathbb{E}_e$ averages over the labs'.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="k_C">How many times the 2025 field's experiment compute the labs explore with: $C_e / C_s$.</Symbol>

### Reviewer Budget

<Symbol tex="q">Share of the labs' papers, by count, that get checked. In Compartmentalization at most $q_p$, as only planned rounds write papers.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="W" unit="FTE">Verifiers the labs would need, working full time, to check every paper.</Symbol>

### Top Papers

<Symbol tex="\chi_i">1 if the verifiers check paper $i$, 0 if not.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\psi">Share of the compute held by the papers the verifiers check, when they check the top share $q$ of the papers they choose from: $\psi(q)$. In Results Verification they choose from all of the papers, and re-runs cost $\kappa_c \psi C_e / M$. In Compartmentalization they choose from the planned papers: $\psi(q / q_p)$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="K" under="Top Papers" unit="AIFM research units">Research effort of a paper with the weakest idea, $v_m$, and the median compute, $\tilde c$. Any paper's is $RE_i = K e^{\varepsilon_i / \alpha_v} e^{\sigma_y \xi_i}$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\tilde c" unit="H100e-years">The median paper's compute: $\bar c\, e^{-\sigma_c^2 / 2}$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\varepsilon_i">Paper $i$'s idea term: an exponential draw with mean 1, with $v_i = v_m e^{\varepsilon_i / \alpha_v}$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\xi_i">Paper $i$'s compute term: a standard normal draw, with $c_i = \tilde c\, e^{\sigma_c \xi_i}$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\sigma_y">Spread of the compute term of $\ln RE_i$: $\zeta_p \sigma_c$, as research grows with compute to the power $\zeta_p$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="u">The bar: the verifiers check the papers with $\ln RE_i$ at least $u$ above $\ln K$. The reviewer budget sets it through $q = G(u, \alpha_v)$, in Compartmentalization through $q / q_p$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="G">Share of the papers above the bar $x$ when the idea term has rate $a$: the tail of $\varepsilon_i / a + \sigma_y \xi_i$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="x" under="Top Papers">A bar on $\varepsilon_i / a + \sigma_y \xi_i$, in logarithms of research effort above $\ln K$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="a" under="Top Papers">Rate of the exponential idea term: $\alpha_v$ when papers are counted or weighted by compute, $\alpha_v - 1$ when weighted by research effort.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\Phi">The standard normal distribution function: the chance that a standard normal draw is below its argument.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="\delta">How far a weight $e^{\delta \xi_i - \delta^2 / 2}$ on the papers moves their compute terms $\xi_i$ up.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="f" under="Top Papers">Any function of a paper's compute term $\xi_i$.</Symbol>

<Symbol tex="v" under="Top Papers">A level of idea quality.</Symbol>

### Compute Budget

<Symbol tex="\kappa_c" input="kappa_c">Share of the compute spent producing a paper, $c_i$, that a re-run of its main runs needs. The failed projects the paper carries aren't re-run.</Symbol>

# Workload Approval
