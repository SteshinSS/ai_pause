For sensitivity analysis, we draw tornado plots. We fix all the parameters at their point estimates, move one parameter at a time to its 10th or 90th percentile and see how this changes AI safety work or the number of verifiers to hire.

We do this for only one scenario, the one we think is the most interesting: Compartmentalization, 9 years, at SAR. For AI safety work, we use frozen hiring.
