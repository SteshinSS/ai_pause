Blind compute estimates vs the authors' figures

Each dot is one AI-safety paper whose authors state their compute. Horizontal: the authors' figure; vertical: the model's estimate from the same paper with every compute passage deleted. Both in H100e-hours as AI 2027 counts them (TPP), log scale. The diagonal is a perfect estimate; the band is within ×3; the dark line is the least-squares fit of the log estimate on the log figure. Papers where a model said the text still revealed the compute, or refused, are left out. Hover a dot to see the paper; the same paper is outlined in the other panel. Data: data/blind/dataset.csv.
